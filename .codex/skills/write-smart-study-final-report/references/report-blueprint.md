# Smart Study Thesis Blueprint

## Front matter

1. Title page: student name/ID, exact title, degree, `Undergraduate Thesis Report`, department/university, supervisor, academic year.
2. Declaration using the official current template.
3. Abstract: current target 250-350 words unless confirmed otherwise; one paragraph, no citations, covering problem, need, uniqueness, method/artefact, verified results, contribution, and limitations. Write last.
4. Acknowledgements and optional dedication.
5. Up to eight keywords if required.
6. Contents, list of figures, list of tables, and abbreviations where applicable.

## Main body

### 1 Introduction - approximately 5-7 pages

Cover background, research problem/questions, approved aim/objectives, scope/exclusions, contribution, artefact overview, and report structure.

### 2 Literature Review - approximately 8-10 pages

Review mobile learning, retrieval practice/spaced repetition, AI/NLP question generation and hallucination, collaborative learning, assessment, motivation, and relevant privacy/security. Compare evidence quality and current systems. End with a research gap and design implications.

### 3 Methodology and Artefact Design

Cover research/requirements methods, actual Agile adaptations, traceable requirements, scope/risk/ethics, verified architecture, data and authorization design, API contracts, UI/UX, algorithms, and rejected alternatives.

### 4 Development and Testing

Describe verified implementation by module. Use short annotated original code excerpts, not generic framework definitions. Pair major functions with tests. Test tables should contain ID, preconditions, action/input, expected result, actual result, status, environment, and evidence. Include only testing actually performed.

### 5 Results and Evaluation

Present measured results and observed feedback, then evaluate each objective as achieved, partly achieved, or not achieved. Analyze significance, confidence, limitations, implications, comparison with existing systems, deviations, problems, and unresolved defects. A passing build is not proof of usability, learning improvement, scalability, or AI accuracy.

### 6 Conclusion and Further Work - approximately 2-4 pages

Restate the aim, summarize approach/results, answer research questions, state contribution, map conclusions to evidence, acknowledge limitations, and prioritize future work. Add no new evidence.

## Back matter and presentation

Use consistent Harvard references. Appendices may include the A1 portrait poster, proposal/plan, ethics/declarations, questionnaire and anonymized results, requirement/test matrices, diagrams/screenshots, selected code, user guide, and AI-use declaration.

Use A4, numbered headings, page numbers, cross-references, and legible captioned visuals. Verify the legacy Times New Roman 12 pt, 1.5 spacing, and 4 cm binding-margin rules against current BREO guidance.
